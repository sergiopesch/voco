# Community conduct

VOCO welcomes people of every background and experience level. Be kind, patient
and specific. Critique the work, listen to different perspectives, and help others
learn. Respect privacy and use examples that do not expose somebody else's data.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption
and publishing private information without permission are not welcome.

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests and other
project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, close a
discussion, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the
behavior and protect those affected.

Use GitHub's **Report content** menu to report abuse without reposting sensitive
details. If an incident concerns a maintainer, report it directly to GitHub.
Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting path](SECURITY.md).
