# VOCO 2026.0.57

Complete onboarding and return directly to the tray. If delivery is interrupted,
healthy local transcription now continues through Stop; VOCO sends a notification
and keeps saved text available when you choose to open it. It never retries an
uncertain paste automatically.

The guided installer has a larger silver VOCO wordmark, green completed steps and
amber progress accents. Its Bash and APT views share one checked brand source.
Short and narrow terminals, plain output, reduced motion and native prompts retain
their existing fallback behavior. Animation adds no minimum installation delay.

- [Installer visual and performance checks](../testing/installer-brand-2026-09-23.md)
- [Release qualification](../testing/tray-brand-release-2026-09-23.md)

The recognizer, model and input permissions are unchanged. Sessions retain the
existing ten-minute bound. Recovery remains in memory until copied, discarded or
VOCO closes. Physical microphones and untested desktops remain separate acceptance
checks. Fedora, openSUSE and Arch packages remain at 2026.0.43.
