# VOCO 2026.0.58

Keep your dictated words when pressing Alt+D to finish in Brave's address bar.
On GNOME Wayland, the companion consumes the Stop shortcut and waits for modifier
release. Held Stop requests stay attached to their recording through status
updates; an older gesture cannot stop a replacement recording.

Continuations reject selected text, and the prepared caret and context are checked
again immediately before paste. Intentional selection replacement remains available
when starting a new dictation. If safe delivery cannot be established, local
transcription continues and saved text remains available on request.

The GNOME companion must be active for consuming shortcuts. After installation,
follow any sign-out/sign-in guidance from setup; `voco --check-panel` reports its
status. Without it, selection guards preserve existing text, but completion may
need saved-text recovery.
