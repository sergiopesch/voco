# VOCO 2026.0.64

Dictation is the first step. We're building the voice layer for Linux: private,
local, and under your control.

**Today: private dictation for Linux.** Press a shortcut, speak, and your words
appear where you are typing, while you speak.

On GNOME Wayland, the GNOME panel now presses VOCO's paste keys from inside
GNOME Shell, and its menu can quit VOCO. VOCO can be built as a strict snap for
the Snap Store. The microphone setting shows the system default it uses, and on
X11 a paste no longer swallows a shortcut pressed just then. It runs on the same
systems as 2026.0.63: Ubuntu 24.04 and 26.04, Debian 13 and Fedora 44. The
[changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) lists every change.

## What's new

- **The GNOME panel types for VOCO.** On GNOME Wayland, while the panel is
  connected, it presses VOCO's paste keys from inside GNOME Shell instead of
  VOCO's own virtual keyboard. It sends the same Shift+Insert, never while you
  hold a modifier, and GNOME turns it off at the lock screen. The panel asks VOCO
  for its work, which is also how a confined VOCO can use it. Until GNOME loads
  the new panel, VOCO types through its own keyboard as before.
- **A new name for the panel.** The panel is now
  `voco-panel@sergiopesch.com`, version 18, a name extensions.gnome.org can
  publish. `voco --setup-panel` moves an enabled `voco-panel@voco.local` to it.
- **Quit VOCO from the panel.** The panel's menu, which a right-click opens,
  ends with **Quit VOCO**, as the tray's does. It is greyed out while you
  dictate.
- **The microphone you'll get.** On Wayland the microphone setting shows
  **System default (current device)** until you choose one, which is the one a
  recording uses, rather than **Choose a microphone**.
- **No lost shortcut on X11.** Pressing the shortcut just as VOCO pasted could do
  nothing, because the Shift of VOCO's Shift+Insert turned Alt+D into
  Alt+Shift+D. A paste now waits while a modifier is held.
- **VOCO as a snap.** VOCO can be built as a strict snap, `voco`, from the same
  files as the `.deb` and the RPM. It has no network access, the Snap Store
  updates it, and it types through the GNOME panel on GNOME Wayland and through
  `xdotool` on X11. It goes to the store's `edge` channel first; until the store
  connects its microphone and session permissions on its own, VOCO names the
  `snap connect` commands. The GNOME panel for it comes from
  extensions.gnome.org.
- **Dependencies.** serde_json 1.0.154, icu_normalizer and icu_properties 2.4.0,
  tauri-plugin-global-shortcut 2.4.1, @tauri-apps/api 2.12.4, React 19.3.1 and
  Vite 8.3.5. The packages are built with Rust 1.99.0 and Tauri CLI 2.12.1, as
  2026.0.63 was. The speech model and the speech worker are unchanged.

## Upgrade

Use these steps once the
[2026.0.64 release page](https://github.com/sergiopesch/voco/releases/tag/voco.2026.0.64)
lists the signed packages.

1. Finish any dictation, and copy anything you need from Review.
2. Quit VOCO with **Quit VOCO** in the tray menu. The GNOME panel of 2026.0.63
   has no quit item, so with the panel run `pkill -x voco`.
3. Run this release's guided installer, or repeat the
   [manual install](../install.md#manual-install):

   ```bash
   wget -qO voco-install https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sergiopesch/voco/voco.2026.0.64/install && bash voco-install
   ```

4. On GNOME, run `voco --setup-panel`, then sign out and back in, so GNOME loads
   the panel's new name and version. Until then VOCO types through its own
   keyboard, and Alt+D also reaches the app you dictate into.

Your settings are kept.

## Known limits

- Dictation replaces what the clipboard and the primary selection hold.
- My words makes VOCO lean toward your words but can't guarantee them: one that
  sounds exactly like another, or that VOCO hears as a different word, can still
  come out wrong. **Say it** shows what VOCO hears, and **Teach VOCO** fixes a
  version it gets wrong.
- A taught version made only of everyday words changes those words every time
  you say them.
- Where you pause between sentences, the full stop often comes a word late or
  not at all, with no capital after it. VOCO doesn't add one, because it would
  also end sentences where you only pause to think
  ([the measurements](../../research/pause-sentence-ends-2026-10/README.md)).
- After a pause, a rich-text field can make the next space a no-break space, at
  most one for each pause, and may keep the space Stop adds as one.
- On Wayland, session checks are not an atomic lock: switching sessions after a
  check can still redirect keys. An unavailable logind status permits pasting.
  See the [input-device security model](../security/README.md#input-devices).
- GNOME's own microphone indicator can still shift the top bar's icons while the
  microphone is in use.
- On Debian 13 and Fedora 44, VOCO has an icon in the top bar only once the GNOME
  panel is on, because they don't turn on an AppIndicator extension.
- DNF says it skipped OpenPGP checks for the RPM file. The installer verifies the
  signed checksums first, which is what authenticates the package.
- VOCO discards a recording it can't confirm is complete, and says so at Start.
- When it pastes, VOCO can't tell a password field from other fields. Don't
  dictate into one. The Chromium extension refuses password fields.
- Don't dictate while a terminal program is waiting for an answer, such as a
  coding agent's permission prompt: it may read your words as typed keys. See
  [Where your words go](../everyday-use.md#where-your-words-go).
- VOCO knows the screen is locked only when the desktop reports it to logind, as
  GNOME does.
- Apps that remap Shift+Insert, remote desktops and virtual machines may not
  accept the paste. Custom editors and other compositors need their own testing.
- On Wayland, a shortcut other than Alt+D or Alt+Shift+D works only when you bind
  it to `voco --toggle` in your desktop's keyboard settings.
- In the snap on X11, a microphone permission granted after VOCO started takes
  effect only after a restart; VOCO says so.
- VOCO recognizes English only. It needs x86-64 with AVX2, FMA and F16C, and
  glibc 2.39 or later.
