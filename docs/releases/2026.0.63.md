# VOCO 2026.0.63

Dictation is the first step. We're building the voice layer for Linux: private,
local, and under your control.

**Today: private dictation for Linux.** Press a shortcut, speak, and your words
appear where you are typing, while you speak.

This release lets you check how VOCO hears the words on your My words list and
teach it the versions it gets wrong, and setup now offers My words. Browser
address bars keep every space, GNOME apps get each paste sooner, and the guided
installer finishes on Ubuntu 26.04. It runs on the same systems as 2026.0.62:
Ubuntu 24.04 and 26.04, Debian 13 and Fedora 44. The
[changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) lists every change.

## What's new

- **Say it and Teach VOCO.** On the **My words** page, choose **Say it** beside
  a word and say it the way you usually do. VOCO shows what it heard, in its own
  window only. If it heard something else, **Teach VOCO** saves that version,
  and dictation writes your word wherever VOCO hears it. VOCO keeps up to five
  versions of each word, and asks you to say it again before teaching one made
  only of everyday words.
- **My words in setup.** After the voice test, setup offers My words, with the
  name on your account as the first word to check. Anything else is yours to
  add.
- **Every space in address bars.** In the address bar of Chrome, Brave, Edge and
  other Chromium browsers, words you say after a pause no longer run into the
  previous word, and rich-text fields, such as an email body, get ordinary
  spaces. While you speak, the last letter waits for your next words, and
  appears when you pause for about a second.
- **A space after a finished sentence.** When a dictation ends a sentence and
  VOCO typed all of it, Stop adds one space, so your next dictation or typing in
  the same field starts apart.
- **Faster typing on GNOME.** VOCO pastes the next words as soon as the app has
  read the last paste, instead of waiting a fixed 150 ms. On a heavily loaded
  desktop it waits, at most 100 ms, for GNOME to take the new text, so a paste
  can't carry the phrase before it.
- **The installer finishes on Ubuntu 26.04.** The installers of 2026.0.62 and
  earlier could stop at **Package setup**, because Ubuntu 26.04's `sudo` stops
  APT there.
- **VOCO recovers when its window crashes.** If the process that draws VOCO's
  window crashes, VOCO loads the window again within a second and keeps what it
  heard in Review, instead of staying at Listening.
- **Settings survive going back a version.** If you try an earlier release and
  then upgrade again, VOCO brings back the settings the earlier release dropped.
- **Sway from a text console.** Sway and other compositors started from a text
  console type through VOCO's virtual keyboard, and the guided installer opens
  VOCO afterwards.
- **More names in My words,** such as the Yoruba Adéṣọ̀lá, with up to three
  accents on a letter.
- **Setup's last step** leads with signing out and back in while Alt+D still
  reaches the app you dictate into, until the GNOME panel loads.
- **What VOCO doesn't do.** A [new page](../scope.md) lists what VOCO leaves out
  on purpose and the limits it has today, and the README qualifies its claims.
- **Evidence you can rerun.** `scripts/check-privacy.sh` measures VOCO's network
  and microphone claims end to end, and `scripts/check-speech.sh` measures
  recognition, latency and My words. The
  [pause study](../../research/pause-sentence-ends-2026-10/README.md) shows why
  VOCO doesn't add full stops where you pause.
- **Dependencies.** icu_normalizer 2.1.1 and icu_properties 2.1.2, for the My
  words rules, and Tokio 1.51.6, past the AsyncFd I/O-safety advisory
  RUSTSEC-2026-0386, which VOCO's code doesn't reach. The packages are built
  with Rust 1.99.0 and Tauri CLI 2.12.1, as 2026.0.62 was. The GNOME panel's
  code and the speech model are unchanged.

## Upgrade

Use these steps once the
[2026.0.63 release page](https://github.com/sergiopesch/voco/releases/tag/voco.2026.0.63)
lists the signed packages.

1. Finish any dictation, and copy anything you need from Review.
2. Quit VOCO with **Quit VOCO** in the tray menu. The GNOME panel has no quit item,
   so with the panel run `pkill -x voco`.
3. Run this release's guided installer, or repeat the
   [manual install](../install.md#manual-install):

   ```bash
   wget -qO voco-install https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sergiopesch/voco/voco.2026.0.63/install && bash voco-install
   ```

Your settings are kept. The GNOME panel's code is the same as in 2026.0.62, so
you don't need to sign out and back in.

## Known limits

- Dictation replaces what the clipboard and the primary selection hold.
- My words makes VOCO lean toward your words but can't guarantee them: one that
  sounds exactly like another, or that VOCO hears as a different word, can still
  come out wrong. **Say it** shows what VOCO hears, and **Teach VOCO** fixes a
  version it gets wrong.
- A taught version made only of everyday words changes those words every time
  you say them.
- Where you pause between sentences, the full stop often comes a word late or
  not at all, with no capital after it. VOCO doesn't add one, because it would
  also end sentences where you only pause to think
  ([the measurements](../../research/pause-sentence-ends-2026-10/README.md)).
- After a pause, a rich-text field can make the next space a no-break space, at
  most one for each pause, and may keep the space Stop adds as one.
- On Wayland, session checks are not an atomic lock: switching sessions after a
  check can still redirect keys. An unavailable logind status permits pasting.
  See the [input-device security model](../security/README.md#input-devices).
- GNOME's own microphone indicator can still shift the top bar's icons while the
  microphone is in use.
- On Debian 13 and Fedora 44, VOCO has an icon in the top bar only once the GNOME
  panel is on, because they don't turn on an AppIndicator extension.
- DNF says it skipped OpenPGP checks for the RPM file. The installer verifies the
  signed checksums first, which is what authenticates the package.
- VOCO discards a recording it can't confirm is complete, and says so at Start.
- When it pastes, VOCO can't tell a password field from other fields. Don't
  dictate into one. The Chromium extension refuses password fields.
- Don't dictate while a terminal program is waiting for an answer, such as a
  coding agent's permission prompt: it may read your words as typed keys. See
  [Where your words go](../everyday-use.md#where-your-words-go).
- VOCO knows the screen is locked only when the desktop reports it to logind, as
  GNOME does.
- Apps that remap Shift+Insert, remote desktops and virtual machines may not
  accept the paste. Custom editors and other compositors need their own testing.
- On Wayland, a shortcut other than Alt+D or Alt+Shift+D works only when you bind
  it to `voco --toggle` in your desktop's keyboard settings.
- VOCO recognizes English only. It needs x86-64 with AVX2, FMA and F16C, and
  glibc 2.39 or later.
