# Research records

Each folder records one study at a date: its question, method, results and
limits, with the harness that produced them and the raw results. A record isn't
kept current; VOCO's [documentation](../docs/README.md) describes how VOCO works
now and links here for the evidence behind a choice.

| Record | Question |
| --- | --- |
| [Speech engine benchmark, October 2026](engine-benchmark-2026-10/README.md) | Should VOCO change its speech engine, or Nemotron's latency setting? |
| [Ending sentences at pauses, October 2026](pause-sentence-ends-2026-10/README.md) | Can the worker end a sentence where you pause, without ending sentences where you pause to think? |
| [Correcting by voice, November 2026](voice-correction-2026-11/README.md) | Can a few words said again, and a fixed rule, change the right words VOCO typed and nothing else? |
